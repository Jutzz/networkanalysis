Bedienungsqualität Layers:
extra_feeddate_method_startdate_enddate

extra: Additional strings explaining the layer, for example no extra would be the standard calculation as mean of multiple days, and something like
"daily", "hourly", "variability"
can be used for additional tables.

feeddate: Publication date of the GTFS-Feed the Bedienugsqualität values are based on
startdate: first date being used for the calculation of Bedienungsqualität
enddate: last date being used for the calculation of Bedienungsqualität
